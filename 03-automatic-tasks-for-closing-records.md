# Chapter 3: Automatic Tasks for Closing Records

## Automatic Tasks for Closing Records

Now that we have transferred our Buyer to Closing, the old Buyer record is retained, but its status is set to "Contract". This is in case the Closing falls through and you need to go back and reactivate the old Buyer record.

Click the "Tasks" tab. You see several automatic tasks.

> Image: Screenshot of the Closing Tasks list with columns Date, Task Description and Categories, each row with an "Edit" link. Tasks include "Buyer needs to Sign Purchase Agreement document" (Nov 29), "Get key from seller" (Nov 29), "Loan Status Pre-Qualified" (Nov 29), "Put up sign" (Nov 29), "Seller Needs to sign Purchase Agreement document" (Nov 29), "Thank you letter" (Nov 29), "Pay commission tax" (Nov 30, AutoTask Commission), "Send housewarming gift" (Nov 30, AutoTask Gift) and "Post-close call (1 day)" (Dec 1, AutoTask PostClosing).

Automatic tasks are generated from what you have in your Parties, General, Sign, Contingencies, Escrow and Documents sections. If you enter the data into RealtyJuggler, Automatic Tasks are generated for you.

### Documents Tab

Some Automatic Tasks relate to signing documents. These tasks are controlled from the "Documents" tab, where you can track who needs to sign which documents.

> Image: Screenshot of the Documents list with columns Type, Info and Dates. One row reads "Purchase Agreement", with a "Download Documents" link beneath it.

> Image: Screenshot of the Document form. Buttons are "Done" and "Delete Document". Fields are Type (Other), Description, "Require Signature From:" (Buyer & Seller), Buyer Received, Buyer Signed, Seller Received, Seller Signed, "Document Expire Date", "Signed Documents Sent", "CC Signed Documents To:" (No One) and a Notes box. Most dates read "No Date".

You can set expire dates for specific documents. If a deal does not require a document, delete that document and the automatic tasks relating to it go away. If there are additional documents you want to track, create new documents and the automatic tasks track signatures, expire dates and so on.

Do not confuse scanned versions of documents with the information you enter on the Documents tab. To upload scanned versions of contracts, place those files in the Notes and Files tab.

You can control the type of document by selecting from the "Type:" drop-down menu. Different fields become available depending on your selection.

### Parties Tab

The tasks relating to Loan Status and Inspections are built from the "Parties" section. Click a particular Party to see detailed information. You can enter address information and up to 9 phone numbers and email addresses. Only 2 are visible at first, with more appearing as needed.

> Image: Screenshot of the Parties list with columns Role, Name, Phone/Email and Last Contact. The roles are Buyer (with a green dot), Seller, Buyer Agent, Buyer Mortgage, Home Inspection Inspector, Insurance, Listing Agent, Settlement/Escrow for and Title Company. A legend reads "=Client". Links at the bottom read "Download Parties" and "Tutorial 18".

The "Role:" drop-down menu lets you specify what role a party plays in your transaction. Selecting the role changes the bottom part of the screen so you can enter information specific to that party.

The Parties section has the Buyer and Seller party at the top of the list. These parties cannot be deleted because they are required for every closing.

If you have several buyers (or sellers) for a deal, you can add additional buyers (or sellers). You can delete only the additional ones, not the first buyer and seller, which are reserved.

> Image: Screenshot of the Buyer Mortgage party form. Role is "Buyer Mortgage", Loan Status is "Pre-Qualified", with fields for Down $, Applied On (No Date), Loan Type (Conventional), Approved For $, Loan #, Loan Expire (No Date), Conditions and Problems.

> Image: Screenshot of the Inspector party form. Role is "Inspector", with Description, Contingency, Appointment Scheduled (No Date, No Time), Report Due Date, Report Received Date, Report Expires Date, Cost $, Paid By (Buyer), Last Contact (No Date) and "Contact again in:" with a days box.

> Image: Screenshot of the Referral party form. Role is "Referral", Agent is checked, Direction is "Received Client Referral (You Pay Other Agent)", with Referral Fee %, Contract (None), Referral Paid (No Date), Thanked (No Date), Last Contact (No Date) and "Contact again in:" with a days box.

You can add parties that are not listed and delete parties that are already there. If you have a favorite set of parties you work with on every deal, see Main:Settings:Defaults, where you can set up defaults for your parties.

### Contingencies Tab

The "Contingencies" tab lets you track all the contingencies for your closing. Automatic Tasks appear in your "Tasks" section for the deadline date and status of each contingency. Once all contingencies have been met, an Automatic Task prompts you to update the "Closing Status:" to "Pre-Close", meaning all necessary conditions have been met prior to closing.

> Image: Screenshot of the Contingencies list with columns Due and Description and Status. One row reads "Mortgage" with the status "Unknown".

> Image: Screenshot of the Contingency form. Buttons are "Done" and "Delete Contingency". Fields are Description (Mortgage), Status (Unknown), Deadline (No Date), Document (a checkbox) and a Notes box.

You can add notes to each contingency to track progress. The "Document" checkbox means there is an associated document for that contingency.

You can generate a set of default contingencies for all your deals at Main:Settings:Defaults. You can then delete (and add) contingencies case by case for each deal. For example, by default you probably want a Mortgage contingency. If your buyer is paying cash, delete the contingency for that deal.

> Image: Screenshot of a task list showing contingency tasks with the category AutoTask: "Seller needs to Sign Purchase Agreement document", "Mortgage contingency Unknown", "Nov 27, 2009 Radon contingency Pending", "Loan status None" and "Oct 1, 2009 Attend Closing" (in bold red as past due). A footer note says dates that are bold and red are past due.

For a Short Sale, enter "Lender Approval of Short Sale" as a contingency and mark its status as a "Deal Blocker". Until all "Deal Blocker" contingencies are met, other tasks, such as inspections and other contingencies, are put on hold.

### Escrow Tab

The Escrow tab lets you track planned escrow payments and which payments have been made. It also tracks when disbursements have been made. Automatic tasks are generated from what you enter in the Escrow section. This is particularly handy if a deal requires more than one payment on a specific date. The total escrow balance is listed on this tab, and Automatic Tasks are generated to close that balance out prior to closing.

> Image: Screenshot of the Escrow tab with buttons "Done", "New Deposit $" and "Close Deposit". A table with columns Date, Activity, Planned $ and Actual $ shows one row: Nov 30, Deposit, $5,000.00 planned, $5,000.00 actual. Below are "Planned Balance: $5,000.00" and "Actual Balance: $5,000.00" and a "Download Earnest Money Deposit" link.

To create a new earnest payment or withdrawal, click the "New Earnest $" button to see a detailed view.

> Image: Screenshot of the Deposit form with buttons "Done" and "Delete Deposit $". Fields are Activity (Deposit), Date (today), Planned $ (5000), Actual $ (5000), Check # (123), Bank # (Bank of America) and a Notes box.

### Status of a Closing Record

Closings start with a status of "Under Contract". If the deal falls through, you can change the status to "Cancelled".

> Image: Screenshot of the Closing status fields. "Close Status:" is a drop-down set to "Closed", with "Extra Status:" below it and "Property Address:" containing "813 Melody court".

Once you have met all Contingencies and set a close date, an automatic task prompts you to set the status to "Pre-Close". Once the Closing date has passed, you are prompted to change the status to "Closed".

If you represented the Buyer and added the Buyer to the Contacts database with the "Add To Contacts" menu item, an AutoTask reminds you to update the Buyer's Home Address. Click the body of that task to go to the Contact record of your Buyer. At the bottom of the Address tab, a drop-down lets you update the Home Address automatically from the Property Address. RealtyJuggler also asks whether you want to add a closing Anniversary to the contact record.

> Image: Screenshot of a task list with columns Date, Task Description and Categories: "Oct 30, Pay commission tax, AutoTask Commission"; "Oct 30, Send housewarming gift, AutoTask Gift"; "Oct 31, Post-close call (1 day), AutoTask PostClosing"; "Nov 29, Buyer needs to Sign Purchase Agreement document, Edit"; and "Nov 29, Get key from seller, Edit". Oct 30 and Oct 31 dates are in red.

Once you have picked up your commission check, you are asked to set the status to "Complete".

After closing, a few tasks appear for follow-up calls to your client. They depend on your preference settings at Main:Settings:Preferences:Tasks. By default you are reminded to contact your Buyer after 1 day, 7 days and 30 days, and then annually on the closing anniversary. Many agents like to make these follow-up calls, so RealtyJuggler makes it easy. If this is not your style, uncheck these tasks in your preference settings (Main:Settings:Preferences:Tasks).

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Under Contract | Buyer and Seller have an agreement. Contingencies have NOT yet been met. In the case of a Short Sale, the lender has NOT yet given approval. |
| Pre-Close | All Contingencies have been met. If a Short Sale, the lender has agreed to the deal. |
| Closed | The closing has taken place. |
| Complete | The deal is complete, you have updated your client's new home address information, and you have been paid your Commission. |
| Gave Referral | This is not your deal, but you gave a referral to another agent and have a financial interest in the deal outcome. A record with this status is a handy way to track the progress of the deal, and your Referral Fee and when it has been paid. |
| Cancelled | The deal has failed. You may want to hold on to this record for a while, just in case the deal is revived. |

### Extra Status Values for Buyer, Listing, Closing and Rental Records

If you need an additional Status value for your Closing record (or Buyer, Listing or Rental), enter it in the "Extra Status:" field. You can edit the drop-down list of Extra Status values at Main:Settings:Preferences:General.

The Contacts, Calendar, Expenses and Prospects databases use Categories for the same purpose. Edit Categories at Main:Settings:Preferences.
