# Chapter 4: Automatic Tasks for Listing Records

## Automatic Tasks for Listing Records

Return to our old Prospect record. Suppose our Prospect needs to sell their current home and purchase another, and they have decided to use us for both transactions. We have already created the Buyer record, so now we need to create a Listing record.

> Image: Screenshot of a task list with columns Due/Done, Task Description and Categories. Rows include "Put up sign" (AutoTask Sign), "Seller needs to Sign Listing Agreement document" (AutoTask Document), "Enter Listing Agreement Date" (AutoTask, dated Dec 6, 2013), "Seller needs to sign Listing Agreement document", "Take pictures" (AutoTask Pictures), "Install lockbox" (AutoTask Lockbox) and "Get key from Seller" (AutoTask Key).

The Categories for the Prospect are set to "Client", so we have not been bothered with any automatic tasks for this record.

Click the "Transfer" tab, then the "Listing" icon. Click "OK" when asked whether you want to delete the Prospect record.

You are now in the Main:Listings:General section of RealtyJuggler. Click the Tasks tab to see the tasks automatically generated for our Listing record. These tasks walk you through organizing your lockbox, sign and other particulars.

### Sign Tab

The Sign tab has information about your yard sign. Automatic tasks help you track when to put up the sign and take it down. If you include brochures with your sign, you can track the last time you refreshed the box with flyers and how many flyers are left. Automatic tasks help keep track of these things as well.

> Image: Screenshot of the Sign tab. Buttons are "Done" and "Add Rider". Fields are "Use No Lockbox" and "Use No Sign" checkboxes, Sign Up (No Date), Sign Down (No Date), "# Brochures", "as of" (No Date), "refill every" days, Expense $ and a Notes box. Below is a "Select multiple rows" link and a list of riders with columns Sign Rider, Date Up and Date Down: Sold, Pending, Open House and Home Warranty. The Date Up for the first row is Nov 29.

> Image: Screenshot of the Rider form with buttons "Done" and "Delete Rider". Fields are Sign Rider (Pending), Description, Date Up (No Date), Date Down (No Date) and a Notes box.

You can track riders by working with the list of Riders in the Sign tab. If you have an Open House promotion (Promotions tab), you are reminded to put up and take down the Open House rider at the appropriate times. If you have added a Home Warranty Party (Parties tab), you are reminded to add a Home Warranty rider.

### Lockboxes and Automatic Tasks

> Image: The Lockboxes icon, a dark padlock with the label "Lockboxes" beneath it.

An Automatic Task asks you to "Install Lockbox" for your Listing. Click the body of that task to go to the Main:Listings:General section of RealtyJuggler. There you can check "No Lockbox:" if your listing has no lockbox. If it does, follow the steps below to attach a lockbox to your Listing record.

Enter all your lockboxes in the Lockboxes section of RealtyJuggler:

1. Go to the Main section of RealtyJuggler and click the Lockboxes icon.
2. Click the "New Lockbox" button at the top of the screen.
3. Enter information about your lockbox and click Done.
4. Repeat for every lockbox in your inventory.

> Image: Screenshot of the Lockbox form. Buttons are "Done", "Delete Lockbox" and "Duplicate Lockbox". Fields are Property Address, Property MLS #, Lockbox Type (None), Shackle, CBS Code, Hours and a Notes box.

Link a lockbox to your Listing:

1. Go to the Main section of RealtyJuggler and click the Listings icon.
2. Click your Listing record.
3. Click the "LockBox #" link to bring up a yellow drop-down menu.
4. Select "LookUp Lockbox". A screen appears with your Lockbox inventory. Click the lockbox you want to associate with this listing by clicking its row.
5. You then see the lockbox associated with your listing. A blue double arrow >> is displayed to the right. Click it to go to the Lockbox record.

> Image: Screenshot of the lockbox fields on a Listing. A "No Lockbox:" checkbox is followed by "Lockbox #" and "Occupied:" labels with arrows, and a yellow drop-down menu listing "LookUp Lockbox" and "Help".

> Image: Screenshot of the lockbox lookup list with columns "Lockbox #" and Address, showing one row: 65664-775, 123 main street.

> Image: Screenshot of the linked lockbox field showing "No Lockbox:" unchecked and "Lockbox # 65664-775" with a blue >> arrow to the right.

### Promotions Tab

The Promotions tab lets you track all the promotions you have planned for your listing. You can schedule Open Houses, advertisements and any other promotions here. Automatic tasks keep you on track with these promotions.

> Image: Screenshot of the Promotions list with buttons "Done" and "New Promotion". A "Select multiple rows" link sits above a table with columns Date, Description and Cost $, showing one row: Flyer, $50.00. Below is "Total Cost: $50.00".

To create a new promotion, click the "New Promotion" button at the top of the screen. Select from the "Promotion Type:" drop-down menu what kind of promotion you will track. The fields displayed change with the type.

> Image: Screenshot of the Promotion form. Buttons are "Done", "New Promotion" and "Delete Promotion". Fields are Promotion Type (Flyer), Description, Ordered On (Dec 2, 2016), Promotion Date (No Date, No Time), # Copies (500), Cost $ (50), Vendor and a Notes box.
