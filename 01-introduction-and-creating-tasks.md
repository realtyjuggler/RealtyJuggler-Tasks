# Chapter 1: Introduction and Creating Tasks

## Introduction

RealtyJuggler can assign tasks to your deals, Contacts and Prospects. You can assign a bundle of tasks, called a Task Plan, to a deal, or automatically assign tasks whenever you create a new record.

RealtyJuggler also has Automatic Tasks. These are generated (and deleted) automatically based on the information you enter in your records. When the information is updated, the tasks update automatically. For example, if you have a Closing and have not yet set its "Close Date:", you have an automatic task in your Tasks tab asking you to set the Close Date. Once you set the Close Date, that task goes away. Automatic Tasks help you keep track of deadlines and other important responsibilities. They help you work your deals and make sure you do not forget anything obvious, like a listing expire date.

This document walks you through using RealtyJuggler tasks. Print it out, start at the beginning and follow along in your own RealtyJuggler account. By the end of this tutorial you will have a firm understanding of how to use Tasks.

## Tracking Birthdays and Anniversaries

> Image: The Contacts icon, a dark-blue circle containing a white star, with the label "Contacts" beneath it.

Sign into RealtyJuggler and click the "Contacts" icon. Click the "New Contact" button at the top of the screen. This creates a new contact and puts you in the "General" tab (the tabs are along the left side of the window). Enter your contact's information. About halfway down the page are two date fields for Birthday and Anniversary. The date ("No Date") is a blue link. Set your contact's Birthday or Anniversary by clicking that "No Date" link.

> Image: Screenshot of part of the contact General tab. Two drop-downs, "B-Day" and "Anniversary", sit above rows for Google Drive Folder, Web Site, Instant Message, Sports, Hobbies, Children, Referred By, Last Contact and "Contact every:". A "Pick Date" calendar pop-up for November (with Today, No Date and Cancel buttons) overlays the form.

To enter two birthdays (for a husband and wife, for example), change the second drop-down menu to "B-Day", just like the first.

> Image: Screenshot of two date rows. The first drop-down reads "B-Day" with the date link "Nov 29"; the second reads "Anniversary" with the link "No Date".

If you have more than two special annual dates for a single contact, do not worry. Once you fill in the first two, additional dates appear. You can enter up to 9 annual dates per contact record.

From the drop-down menu to the left of the date, select what kind of annual date you are entering. For some dates (such as a child's birthday or closing anniversary) you can enter additional information, such as the child's name, in the "for:" text field immediately below. For a Closing anniversary, enter the address and year of the closing. For a child's birthday, enter the child's first name and year of birth.

> Image: Screenshot of a "Child B-Day" row with the date link "Dec 10", and below it a "for:" field containing "Billy 2004".

Now click the "Tasks" tab on the left of the screen.

> Image: Screenshot of the contact tab list: General (selected), Address (1), Photo, Service Report, Mail, Mailing Label, Tasks (1), Appointments, Backlinks and Transfer.

The upper-left corner shows where you are: Main:Contacts:Tasks. That means you are in the contacts section looking at the tasks tab. Click any piece of that text to jump to that location. Clicking "Main" takes you back to the Main section of RealtyJuggler.

The upper-right corner shows a description of the record you are viewing. Since you are working on a contact record for yourself, you see your own name there. Click the name to go directly to the "General" tab.

The Tasks list already has one task, for the Birthday you entered in the "General" section.

> Image: Screenshot of the contact Tasks tab. Buttons at the top are "Done", "Apply Task Plan" and "New Task". Filter links read "Completed Tasks: Hide", "Tasks with No Due Date: Show" and "AutoTasks: Show". One task row shows a checkbox, the date "Dec 10", the description "Child B-Day 2004 Reminder", a name and address, and the categories "AutoTask" and "B-Day". Links at the bottom read "Add Label to Queue", "View All Tasks", "Edit Task Plans", "Download Tasks", "Tasks Tutorial", "Tutorial 38" and "Tutorial 39".

The task's category is AutoTask and B-Day. That means the task is generated automatically from the information you enter. Birthday and Anniversary reminders appear as Tasks. Check a task done by clicking its checkbox, and the reminder is bumped up 1 year to next year's birthday. Many agents like to call their contacts on their Birthday and Anniversary, and this is an easy way to do it.

To turn off this automatic task, go to Main:Settings:Preferences:Tasks and uncheck the "Contact B-Day Reminders:" check-box. Then no automatic tasks are generated for Birthdays and Anniversaries.

You cannot delete automatic tasks. They are generated from the information you enter (in this case the Birthday) and the preference settings you have set.

Suppose you send birthday and anniversary cards once a week. To see all Birthdays and Anniversaries for the upcoming week, click the All Tasks icon in the Main part of RealtyJuggler, click the Week tab along the top, then choose B-Day from the filter drop-down in the upper-right corner. This displays all Birthdays and Anniversaries for all your Contacts due within the next week.

### Microsoft Outlook Birthday and Anniversary Alarms

RealtyJuggler reminds you of Contact Birthdays and Anniversaries using Auto Tasks, which lets you see the reminders and check them as done. Microsoft Outlook takes a slightly different approach and automatically generates a repeating Appointment when you set the Birthday or Anniversary field for an Outlook Contact record. If you synchronize with Microsoft Outlook, you may see these reminders in both places. The two approaches coexist without conflict.

## Let's Create a Task

Birthday and Anniversary fields keep you in touch with contacts twice a year. For a close circle of contacts you talk to more often, create a repeating task. Here we create a task that reminds us to call a person every 3 months.

Start with the contact record you created. Click the "Tasks" tab, then the "New Task" button at the top of the screen. Several fields are available. Type "Call Rachael" as the task description. There is no need to enter the phone number because the task is attached to the contact record.

> Image: Screenshot of the New Task form. Buttons at the top are "Done" and "Delete Task". Fields are Task Description, a drop-down "Client/Prospect" labeled "to Task Description" with red text "When this task is marked done, Last Contact date will be updated", Categories, Fixed Due Date (today), Repeat, every, Stop Repeating, "Repeat New Due Date Calculated from:", Email Alarm, Done, Date Completed and a Notes box.

> Image: Screenshot of the contact Tasks list showing a task with the description "All tasks must have a description" and the date "Dec 10", with "Edit" and "Add Label to Queue" links.

The "Categories" field organizes your tasks. If you work in a team, you can assign the task to a team member by typing their name in that field. Leave it blank for now.

Since you are creating the task, leave the "Done" checkbox blank. "Date Completed" should also be blank. We use a fixed due date of today, so leave those two fields alone.

Next, choose Monthly from the "Repeat" drop-down menu. Additional fields appear, and you can fill in the number 3 for repeating every 3 months.

The "Repeat New Due Date from:" field is a little tricky. There are two choices:

- "Old Due Date": once you mark the task done, a new task is generated with a due date 3 months after the old due date. Use this to call someone on June 3rd every year, the day their child was born.
- "Old Date Completed": the new task has a due date three months after the day you marked the task done. Use this for 3-month intervals. If you call a few weeks early, the timer starts from the date of the last call.

When you finish, click the "Done" button at the top of the screen. The task is created and you return to the Tasks screen, where you see the task in the list.

## Adding Phone Numbers/Mailing Address to a Task

For some tasks, such as calling the person or sending a handwritten letter, you may want to include a phone number or mailing address.

> Image: Screenshot of a drop-down reading "Add Nothing" next to the label "to Task Description".

To enable this, find the "Add Nothing" drop-down next to Task Description and change it to Add Phone Number(s) or Add Mailing Address. You can then select whose contact information to include. The default is your client's information, but you can include any party associated with a deal, such as a loan officer or the opposing realtor.

> Image: Screenshot of the task form with buttons "Done" and "Delete Task". The drop-down "Add Mailing Address from" is set to "Client/Prospect" and labeled "to Task Description", with red text "When this task is marked done, Last Contact date will be updated." The category is "Postcard_1" and the fixed due date is "Dec 28, 2016".

## Printing Labels from the Tasks List

If you selected "Add Mailing Address from" for your task, the mailing address appears in your Task list. To print the label, click the "Add Label to Queue" link right below the address. You can click as many addresses as you like. When you are ready to print, go to Main:Mail:Labels & Envelopes and select "Items in Your Label Queue" from the "-- Select Labels --" drop-down. You can print sheets of labels, print to a label printer, print directly on a #10 envelope, or print onto 4-up postcards.

> Image: Screenshot of a task row with the date "Dec 28", the description "All tasks must have a description", a boxed mailing address "Helen & Steve Adams, 4 Pleasure Road, San Antonio, Texas 78296", the category "Postcard_1" and an "Add Label to Queue" link beneath.

One way to organize label printing is to use task categories. Enter the name of the postcard design as a category for each postcard-printing task. When you are ready to print labels, go to Main:All Tasks and choose from the "Show Category:" menu the category for the postcard design you want. This keeps all the labels for a specific postcard design together.

## Setting an Alarm for a Task

To be notified of a task by email, select from the "Email Alarm:" drop-down. If a task is due on the 16th of June and you set the alarm to "1 Day Before Due Date", you receive an email around 12 midnight on June 15th.

> Image: Screenshot of the task form's lower fields with the "Email Alarm:" drop-down open. The options are None, Sent Early Morning of Due Date, Sent Early Morning 1 Day Before Due Date (selected), and Sent Early Morning 2, 3, 4 and 5 Days Before Due Date. Above it are fields for Fixed Due Date (tomorrow), Repeat (Monthly), every (3 Month(s)), Stop Repeating (Never) and "Due Date Calculated from:" (Old Date Completed).
