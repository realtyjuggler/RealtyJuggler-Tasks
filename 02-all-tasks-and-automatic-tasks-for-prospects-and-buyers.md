# Chapter 2: All Tasks and Automatic Tasks for Prospects and Buyers

## All Tasks

> Image: The All Tasks icon, a black checkmark with the label "All Tasks" beneath it.

So far we have created tasks inside an individual record. The "Tasks" tab is available for Contacts, Prospects, Buyers, Listings, Closings and Rental records. Generally, put tasks that relate to a deal in that deal's record (a Closing, for example) and tasks of a personal nature in the Contact record. That way your tasks always have context attached.

Sometimes you want to see all the tasks for all your deals. Click the "Main" part of the Main:Contacts:Tasks text in the upper-left corner to return to the Main section, then click the "All Tasks" icon. This section displays all your tasks for all your records, with several options to filter and sort them.

> Image: Screenshot of the All Tasks screen. The left menu shows Print, Search All, Contacts, Calendar, All Tasks (selected), Prospects, Buyers and Listings. At the top right is a "Show Category:" drop-down set to "All" and a large black checkmark. Buttons read "Done" and "New Global Task". Filter links are "Completed Tasks: Hide", "Tasks with No Due Date: Show" and "AutoTasks: Show". Date tabs read Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, 1 Year and All (selected). A task row reads "Nov 29, All tasks must have a description, Rental".

- In the upper-right corner, use the "Showing Category:" drop-down menu to filter tasks by category.
- Use the tabs at the top of the screen to filter tasks by due date.
- Hide completed tasks and Automatic tasks using the checkboxes at the top of the screen.

> Image: Screenshot of a task list with columns Date, Task Description, Record Info and Categories. Rows: "Nov 29, All tasks must have a description, Rental, Edit"; "Nov 29, Enter Listing Agreement Date, Listing, AutoTask, Edit"; and "Nov 29, Enter Listing Agreement Date, Listing, Adams, Helen, 123 main street, AutoTask".

You can do two things with your tasks. First, check a task done by clicking the checkbox to the left of the description. Second, view detailed information about the task by clicking its text.

For an Automatic Task, you go to the tab that relates to that Automatic Task. For tasks you created, click the edit link to edit the task, or click the task text to go to a screen that lets you act on it.

There may be tasks that have nothing to do with a particular deal or contact. Click the "New Global Task" button on this screen to create one.

> Image: Screenshot of two buttons, "Done" and "New Global Task".

Tip: When you are first learning about Tasks, keep the two checkboxes "Hide Completed Tasks" and "Hide Automatic Tasks" unchecked, so you can see what is going on with your tasks and the automatic tasks. Once you are comfortable with task management, these checkboxes are an excellent way to filter your tasks.

Tip: To refresh the tasks list and see which tasks have been marked done, click the "Tasks" tab on the left of the screen. That saves any tasks marked done and recalculates Automatic Tasks.

## Automatic Tasks

Automatic Tasks are generated from the data you enter and from what needs to be done to close a deal. Each Automatic Task brings you closer to closing a deal.

> Image: Screenshot of a task list with "Done", "Apply Task Plan" and "New Task" buttons. The tasks listed, all dated Nov 29, are "Get key from seller", "Put up sign", "Thank you letter" and "Post-close call (1 day)", with the last carrying the categories AutoTask and PostClosing.

The intent of Automatic Tasks is to be unobtrusive and to point out what needs to be done and when. The best way to explain them is to walk through a deal and see what happens.

Automatic tasks can usually be marked done by checking the task off. For example, in the list above you could check off the "Put Up Sign" Auto Task. Marking it done makes RealtyJuggler update the "Sign Up:" date field in the Main:Listings:Sign section with today's date. The automatic task then goes away when you refresh the screen (click the Tasks tab on the left to refresh).

If there is no checkbox to the far left of an Automatic Task, click the task text to go to the appropriate section of RealtyJuggler. Make the appropriate changes to the RealtyJuggler fields and the Auto Task goes away.

### Automatic Tasks for EmailFeed

An AutoTask called "CallNow" is added to prospect records created through the Email Feed. Mark this task done and the "CallNow" category is removed and the Last Contact date is updated.

### Automatic Tasks for Prospect Records

From the "Main" section of RealtyJuggler, click the "Prospects" icon, then the "New Prospect" button at the top of the screen. Type in the contact information for your Prospect. Note that the mailing address can differ from the home address.

If you have contact information for the Prospect in your Contacts database, click the text label "First Name:". This brings up a drop-down menu where you can select "LookUp Contact", then click the contact you want to use as your Prospect.

> Image: Screenshot of a First Name field with a yellow drop-down menu showing "LookUp Contact", "Add to Contacts" and "Help".

A blue double arrow >> then appears to the right of the first name field. Click it to view the Contact record you are using for your Prospect.

Now fill in the "Contact Every __" field with the number 12. This means you intend to call this prospect every 12 days. It is a repeating reminder. For a single reminder, select "Contact again in:" from the drop-down menu.

> Image: Screenshot of two small controls. The first is a "Contact every:" drop-down with 12 and "days". The second is a "Contact again in:" drop-down with 5 and "days".

Next, click the "Tasks" tab on the left. You should see a single task reminding you to contact the Prospect. Click the task done, then click the "Tasks" tab again to refresh the screen and recalculate the Automatic Tasks.

> Image: Screenshot of an enlarged task row with a checkbox, the date "Dec 4", the description "Contact Prospect: Anderson, Scott (503) 555-1212 (Home) (Mobile)" and the categories "AutoTask" and "Touch".

Notice that the "Contact every __ days" Automatic Task does not go away. Even when you mark it done, it comes back, because this automatic task makes sure you contact your Prospect at that interval (every 14 days in this example). To stop being reminded, empty the Contact Every __ field.

When you are ready to contact your Prospect, click the body of the task. This takes you to detailed information on the Prospect and gives you space for notes. Make your call and type your notes. Before you get off the phone, schedule your follow-up call by updating the Last Contact date or the Contact every __ values. The Automatic Task is then recalculated with your new values. To add a time-and-date-stamped note, click the yellow note icon in the upper-left corner of the screen.

> Image: Screenshot of the Prospect's Transfer tab. The left tab list shows General, Parties (1), Rent-vs-Own, Max Loan, Payments, Service Report, Mail, Mailing Label, Notes & Files, Tasks (1), Appointments, Backlinks and Transfer (selected). The main area reads "Transfer this record to a new phase by clicking on one of the icons below. In most cases, you will want to delete your old Prospect after the transfer." Icons are Contact, Buyer, Listing, Buyer Closing, Seller Closing and Rental. At the bottom is "Move This Prospect to Another Account" with "Move To:" and "Send a Duplicate To:" drop-downs set to "-- Select Destination --".

Next, click the "Transfer" tab on the left, then the "Buyer" icon. This creates a new Buyer record based on your Prospect record. You are asked whether you want to delete the Prospect record. Normally you would click "OK", but here click "Cancel", because we will use that Prospect record again later.

### Automatic Tasks for Buyer Records

You are now in the Main:Buyers:General section of RealtyJuggler. The information you entered in the Prospect record has been copied to the new Buyer record.

Click the Parties tab. You see two parties: one for your Buyer and a second for the loan officer. Click the "Mortgage" party and enter information about the loan. If you do not want to track that information, or your client is not using a loan, delete the party by clicking the Mortgage line and then the "Delete Party" button.

> Image: Screenshot of the Parties list with columns Role, Name and Phone/Email. The first row is Buyer, with a green dot, "Abbott, Craig, Spouse: Abigail, 15711 Park Way, Tualitan, Idaho 97070" and "(555) 434-8294 (Abigail), CraigAbbottMD@gmail.c...". Below it are Buyer Mortgage and Referral rows. A legend reads "=Client".

Click the Tasks tab. You see your repeating "Contact every __ days" task and a new automatic task about the loan status for your Buyer.

> Image: Screenshot of a task row with the date "Date", the description "Pre-qualify for loan" and the categories "AutoTask" and "Loan".

Automatic Tasks depend on the status of your deal record. Click the "General" tab and you will see "Buy Status:" set to "Active". Once you have a contract, set the status to "Contract". If your buyer abandons the house search, set the status to "Inactive". If you transfer a Buyer to Closing, the status of the Buyer record is automatically set to "Contract".

> Image: Screenshot of the Buyer General fields. "Buy Status:" is a drop-down set to "Contract". Below are "Extra Status:" (a blue label with arrow) and "First Name:" containing "Craig".

Most Automatic tasks go away for a Buyer record whose status is Inactive or Contract.

To transfer our Buyer to Closing, click the "Transfer" tab and then the "Buyer Closing" icon.

> Image: The Buyer Closing icon, two shaking hands drawn in dark blue on a light square, labeled "Buyer Closing".

This copies the Buyer information to a new Closing record and sets the Buyer record's status to "Contract". Any unfinished tasks in the buyer record are deleted, since they are no longer necessary. We have entered a new phase, closing a deal, and are no longer looking for the perfect home for our client.

#### Showings and Offers

The Showings tab (and Offers tab) lets you keep track of the homes you have shown your Buyer. You can track what the buyer thought of a home, when it was seen and who the listing agent is.

> Image: Screenshot of the Showing form. Buttons are "Done", "Duplicate Showing" and "Delete Showing". Fields include Sell Contact First Name, Last Name, Company, Email, Mobile Phone, "Has Mailing Address", "Sell Contact is an Agent" (checked), Property Address, Property MLS #, Status (Unknown), Show Date (today), Confirm Show Date, "Last Followed-Up On" (No Date), Interest (Unknown) and a Notes box.

If you enter this information, Automatic Tasks keep you on track. If you have offers, Automatic tasks keep up with expiration dates, counter-offers and the like.

### Creating a CALL List

To create a list of all the people you need to call for the day:

1. Click the All Tasks icon on the Main screen.
2. Select the appropriate tab at the top of the screen, for example Today.
3. Select Show Category: Touch from the drop-down in the upper-right corner of the screen.
4. To print the list, click the Print icon in the upper-left corner of the screen.

> Image: Screenshot of the All Tasks filters. Links read "Completed Tasks: Show", "Tasks with No Due Date: Show" and "AutoTasks: Show". Date tabs read Today (selected), 7 Days, 30 Days, 1 Year and All. A "Select multiple rows" link sits above a task row dated "Nov 29" with the description "Enter Listing Agreement Date", record "Listing" and category "AutoTask".

The resulting list shows everyone you scheduled to call for the day. To schedule a call, use the "Contact every __ days" option described in Automatic Tasks for Prospect Records. Click the body of a task to see a detailed view of the person to call and enter notes.

Touch Tasks are scheduled relative to a record's Last Contact Date. The Last Contact Date is updated automatically when you send emails, print letters, or complete a Birthday or Anniversary reminder task.

> Image: Screenshot of the Touch preferences with six drop-downs, each set to "Update Last Contact Date": When Mail Printed, When Email Sent, When B-Day Task Marked Done, When Anniversary Task Marked Done, When Task with Added Contact Info Marked Done, When SMS Text Message Sent/Received, and When Dialing Phone.

To use Touch Tasks only for phone calls, adjust your Touch Preferences at Main:Settings:Preferences:Touch so the Last Contact Date changes only under the conditions you specify. By default, RealtyJuggler treats any form of communication (phone call, SMS text message or email) as a valid touch.
